Government of Agrizamin · Territorial administration

Agrizamin

The fertile lands of His Grapeness

Agriculture · Shah Sulaman Dehaty

The agricultural land

Agriculture is one of the pillars of the empire and is what allows the rest of our civilization and the royal families to function.

Harvest and livestock

The most important crops are grains because they're easy to transport and can be stored for long periods and used to feed the poor, who are classified as approximately 70% of the population.

The last major portion of agriculture is contributed through livestock. Farmers raise animals for meat, dairy, transportation, leather, and even as pets for Shah Sulaman.

Village life

Most farmers live in small villages close to the fields. A normal farming family works from the morning until sunset during the most important parts of the agricultural year.

Food and festival

The empire is known for its food, including various kinds of tandoori, promising flame shawarma, grilled meats, bread, rice, and the finest grapes in the world, provided to us by His Grapeness.

Agrizamin, Sulaman’s agricultural land: Festival of the Golden Harvest: huge feasts, grape celebrations, livestock fairs, and offerings from the year’s harvest.

About the federation

Life across the empire

Here in the Promethean Empire, every citizen has a responsibility.

The servants of the shah

Directly underneath the rule of the four great shahs, more minor leaders exist within the structure.

The stewardship of royal funds

It is believed in our civilization that Prometheus appreciates the surrendering of the income of the lower classes, granting them blessings and demonstrating his appreciation by transporting the funds to the shahs so that their hard-earned money is in safe hands.

The royal flush toilet

Other technology includes the flush toilet used and kept in the mansion of Shah Aryan Chandra, for his post-visitation evenings after Tandoori Flame.

Fire-powered artillery

The technology we have includes advanced cannon weaponry (fire explosives).

Government

Our empire is organized as a federation of four lands, which is going to be ruled by one of us, the four great shahs. The shahs govern their own territories throughout most of the year, but during every solstice they travel to the capital and assemble before the undying flame, our most sacred symbol of significance.

Since there are four rulers, votes can result in a two-to-two tie. When this happens, we believe that Prometheus demands a divine sacrifice to secure his intervention.

Class system

Here in the Promethean Empire, we maintain a strict social hierarchy for all. Prometheans believe that our civilization functions best when every being understands their responsibility.

Agriculture

Agriculture is one of the pillars of the empire and is what allows the rest of our civilization and the royal families to function. Our empire yields a wide variety of crops, from fruits like grapes to grains and many different types of vegetables suited for the exquisite palate.

Culture

Promethean culture values honor, stability, nobility, and loyalty, with justice in accordance with the shahs’ judgment. Promethean citizens are expected to respect their family, occupation, the laws of the empire, and the royal families.

Religion and worship

Prometheus, the common ancestor of the 4 shahs, is the main and only one worthy of worship. However, the shahs do come close as his descendants.

Technology

A lot of our technological sector is focused on military and capital splendor.

Written language

It has slowly changed into the current language that we speak, greko-persian. Our script of choice is Alfapedo based on Alfavedo from Greece.

Federation news

Speranzo’s warning

One day during a meeting between the four shahs in Promethia, a mysterious statue starts moving and speaking, identifying herself only as Speranzo, who claims to be communicating from the future.

The transmission later spreads throughout the entire empire and causes different groups to begin questioning the system of governance, which was considered taboo. Farmers demand lower quotas for crop yields and more streamlined access to the wealth provided from their work. Shah Shrey's forces begin debating whether their loyalty belongs to the people or to Shah Shrey himself.

The four shahs now face their greatest political crisis. Each needs the other states for the federation to survive, and nobody wants to surrender authority or the resources to solve the problem.

The shahs also cannot agree on whether Speranzo is actually from the future, an enemy attempting to destabilize the empire, or somehow speaking the will of Prometheus.

The Promethean Empire is therefore left on the verge of civil war without a single foreign entity attacking it.
